## Verified by ear (2026-11-28)
`azan.mp3` was checked section by section on the living-room Echo: 102–123 s hayya 'ala al-falah, 123–138 s Allahu akbar, 138–153 s Allahu akbar + la ilaha illa Allah. Nothing comes between the falah and the closing, so it is a standard (non-Fajr) azan, and it is the file the automation plays (secret `azan_url`). `azan_backup.mp3` is unused; its long block after the falah probably contains the Fajr phrase.
